## Notes

- Do not count a post as a target-company post only because the author currently works at a target company. The interview post itself should mention the target company or business group.
- `WXG`, `微信支付`, `光子`, `混元`, `云智`, and `金融科技` are Tencent-related when the surrounding context says Tencent or the search result is under Tencent.
- `飞书` can mean ByteDance unless the context is only a competition or product mention unrelated to the interview company.
- Keep `米哈游` out of the default Xiaomi bucket; it is not Xiaomi. Only include it if the user extends the company list.
